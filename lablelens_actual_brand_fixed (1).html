What "compliant" actually looks like

✕ Common violation
  • Month/year of packing missing
  • Declaration font below 2mm minimum
  • No consumer care email or address
✓ Fully compliant
  • All mandatory fields present
  • Font size meets pack-size minimum
  • Eligible for the LableLens Verified badge

How LableLens works

01
Scan the label
Point your camera at the packaging — front or back label both work.
02
We check the rules
MRP, net quantity, manufacturer details and font size are checked against Rule 2011.
03
See the result
Get a clear pass/fail per declaration, and file a complaint if something's missing.
